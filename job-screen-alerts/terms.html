Terms of use

Job Screen Alerts is a personal application for its account owner's job search. It is not a public job board or a paid service. There is no public signup.

Owner-controlled access

Only connect a Google account you control. Gmail access is used for the alert delivery and duplicate prevention described in the privacy policy. The app sends alerts to the configured owner address and does not submit job applications or contact employers.

Application decisions

The owner decides whether and when to apply. Shortlist scores and personal apply-by targets are aids for prioritization, not confirmed employer deadlines, eligibility guarantees, or employment advice. Check the original posting for current requirements and availability.

Availability and controls

Collection and delivery depend on the server, network, authorization, and source websites. Alerts can be delayed or interrupted, and postings can close before an alert arrives. The owner can stop the worker, revoke Gmail authorization, or use Applied, Pass, and Snooze in the private dashboard to manage reminders.

Questions can be directed to the support email shown on Google's consent screen.
