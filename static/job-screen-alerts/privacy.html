Privacy policy

This policy describes how the personal Job Screen Alerts application uses Google account data. It is operated by its account owner for the owner's job search.

Access and purpose

The app uses gmail.send to send requested job alerts and reminders to the owner. It uses gmail.readonly to confirm the account's email address and search Sent for the exact unique alert reference addressed to the owner. Candidate messages are read to verify their recipient and reference. The read scope can access the mailbox, but the app does not browse unrelated email or use email content for job selection.

Storage and retention

The OAuth refresh credential is stored in a private file on the owner's trusted computer and Azure server. It allows unattended delivery until revoked or expired. The app retains its own prepared alert content, recipient address, delivery message IDs, alert references, and application/reminder state to prevent duplicates and honor Applied, Pass, and Snooze decisions. These records remain until the owner deletes them. Unrelated mailbox content and attachments are not downloaded or retained by the app.

Sharing and protection

Gmail data is exchanged with Google over HTTPS. The owner's Azure host stores the app's private state and authorization; administration and credential transfers use encrypted SSH with restricted file access. The public website and its GitHub repository contain only application information, not credentials, email contents, job history, or the private dashboard.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, shared with job sites or employers, or sent to AI models for training or analysis. The separate job-posting collection workflow may use external extraction services for job descriptions; it does not receive Gmail mailbox data. Human access to Google data is limited to the account owner or maintenance explicitly authorized by that owner.

Your controls

You can stop future Gmail access by removing the app in Google Account connections. Revocation does not delete past emails or local delivery records. The owner can stop the server worker and delete its credential and retained app data; any backups must be handled separately. Applied, Pass, and Snooze control reminders for individual jobs.

Google API data use

Job Screen Alerts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This information website

These static pages use no analytics, advertising, forms, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process visitor IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Contact the app owner through the support email on Google's consent screen for questions or data-deletion requests.
